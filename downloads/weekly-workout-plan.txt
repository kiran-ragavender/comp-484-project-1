MY CALISTHENICS + WEIGHTS PLAN (4-day cycle)
============================================
Push -> Pull -> Legs -> Rest, then repeat.
Alternate Cycle A and Cycle B each time the cycle repeats.

DAY 1 - PUSH
  Main lift (Cycle A): Weighted dips
  Main lift (Cycle B): Band-assisted wall handstand push-ups
  Accessories: Archer push-ups, bench press, straight bar dips, triceps work

DAY 2 - PULL
  Main lift (Cycle A): Weighted pull-ups
  Main lift (Cycle B): Muscle-ups
  Accessories: Ice cream makers, deadlifts, lower back extensions,
               rows, inverted hanging rows

DAY 3 - LEGS
  Main lift (Cycle A): Pistol squats and weighted pistol squats
  Main lift (Cycle B): Heavy barbell squats
  Accessories: Leg extensions, hamstring curls, Bulgarian split squats,
               calf raises, hanging leg raises, L-sits

DAY 4 - REST
  Light walking and stretching.

SETS AND REPS
  Main lift:   4-5 sets x 3-6 reps, rest 2-3 min
  Accessories: 3 sets x 8-12 reps, rest 60-90 sec

Warm up for 10 minutes before every session.
