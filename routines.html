My 4-day training cycle

I train push, pull and legs on three days in a row, then take one rest day, and repeat: push, pull, legs, rest, push, pull, legs, rest.

Because the cycle is 4 days long instead of 7, my training days don't land on the same weekdays every week. That's fine with me – I just follow the cycle and let the calendar move. Each time the cycle repeats, I switch between Cycle A and Cycle B, which have different main lifts, so every main lift comes around once every 8 days.

One full cycle (Cycle A and Cycle B alternate)
Day Focus Cycle A main lift Cycle B main lift Accessories
Day 1 Push Weighted dips Band-assisted wall handstand push-ups Archer push-ups, bench press, straight bar dips, triceps work
Day 2 Pull Weighted pull-ups Muscle-ups Ice cream makers, deadlifts, lower back extensions, rows, inverted hanging rows
Day 3 Legs Pistol squats and weighted pistol squats Heavy barbell squats Leg extensions, hamstring curls, Bulgarian split squats, calf raises, hanging leg raises, L-sits
Day 4 Rest Recovery: light walking and stretching
Per cycle 3 training days + 1 rest day, repeated all year round

Sets, reps and rest

Main lift

4–5 sets of 3–6 heavy reps, resting 2–3 minutes between sets.

Accessories

3 sets of 8–12 reps, resting 60–90 seconds between sets.

Rest day

No lifting. Sleep, eat enough protein and let the muscles recover.
