My training log

From regular gym-goer to muscle-ups and weighted pull-ups: how my training has changed over the past year.

Before – Regular gym training

I was already going to the gym and doing a general mix of machines, dumbbells and cardio, without a real plan. I could move some weight, but when I tried bodyweight exercises I realized how weak I was at controlling my own body.

October 2025 – Day one of calisthenics

I couldn't do a single pull-up or a single dip. My push-ups were weak and my form was sloppy. I started using resistance bands on the pull-up bar and the dip station so I could practice full reps with good form.

December 2025 – First strict dip and pull-up

After weeks of moving to lighter and lighter bands, I did my first dip and my first pull-up with no assistance at all. This was the moment I knew the bands were working.

March 2026 – First pistol squat

Balance was the hardest part. After a lot of practice I finally went all the way down on one leg and stood back up without falling over.

May 2026 – First muscle-up

Months of pull-ups, dips and transition practice paid off: I got my first clean muscle-up, going from hanging below the bar to pressing up on top of it.

July 2026 – Stronger everywhere

Once I started adding weight to my dips and pull-ups, I noticed something I didn't expect: my bench press, deadlift and squat all went up too. Weighted dips carried over to my bench press, and weighted pull-ups built the back and grip strength that helped my deadlift. Weighted calisthenics wasn't just making me better at calisthenics – it was making me stronger overall.

September 2026 – Where I am now

I now do weighted dips and weighted pull-ups with 20–30 kg on a dip belt, and I follow a push, pull, legs, rest cycle that mixes calisthenics with heavy barbell work. See my routine for the full plan.

Next goals

  • Freestanding handstand push-up – no wall and no band.
  • Front lever and planche – the two big static holds in calisthenics.
  • Heavier weighted lifts – keep adding weight to my dips, pull-ups and squats.
